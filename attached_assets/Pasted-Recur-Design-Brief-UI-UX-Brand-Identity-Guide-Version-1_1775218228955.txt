Product Identity

What is Recur?
Recur is a mobile strength training app for adults (30-55) who work out at home with dumbbells. It generates personalized daily workouts that intelligently adapt to your body, schedule, and pain signals.

The Big Idea: Most people don't fail at fitness because they need harder workouts—they fail because they can't stay consistent. Recur helps you train regularly without getting hurt or burned out.

Brand Foundation
Name: Recur
Tagline: Consistency without flare-ups

What "Recur" Means:
1. To happen again repeatedly (your daily training loop)
2. To return (coming back after breaks)
3. Recurring patterns (Push/Pull/Legs rotation)
Brand Personality:
• Supportive, not aggressive - "Welcome back" not "No excuses"
• Intelligent, not clinical - Smart but warm
• Honest about setbacks - Acknowledges breaks and pain as normal
• Long-term focused - Sustainability over intensity
Visual Themes to Explore:
• Cyclical/circular motifs (recurring, returning, loops)
• Wave patterns (consistency, rhythm)
• Growth rings (building over time)
• Warm, grounded colors (earth tones, not neon)
Avoid:
• Lightning bolts, explosions (too aggressive)
• Medical/clinical imagery (we're not rehab)
• Bodybuilder/fitness model imagery (not our audience)
• Harsh angles, aggressive typography
Target User

Demographics
• Age: 30-55
• Gender: Any (slightly male-skewed based on dumbbell training)
• Location: Home-based trainers
• Income: Middle class ($50k-150k)
Psychographics
• Values consistency over intensity
• Has tried multiple fitness apps/programs
• Frustrated by boom-bust cycles (train hard → get hurt → quit → repeat)
• Manages minor aches (old shoulder injury, occasional back pain)
• Busy with work/family but prioritizes health
• Wants to look and feel good long-term
What They Value
• Intelligence (app adapts to them, not one-size-fits-all)
• Honesty (acknowledges real life happens)
• Simplicity (just tell me what to do today)
• Results (steady progress over time)
UI/UX Principles

Design Philosophy
Simple over clever
• Users are mid-workout, sweating, tired
• Big buttons, clear labels, minimal text
• Don't make them think
Supportive over aggressive
• Warm colors, friendly copy
• Celebrate effort, not just results
• Acknowledge setbacks without guilt
Fast over comprehensive
• Quick logging (pre-fill values, easy adjustments)
• Skip optional steps
• Auto-advance where possible
Consistent over flashy
• Recurring visual patterns (matching "recur" brand)
• Predictable layout (same place for same actions)
• Smooth but not distracting animations
Interaction Patterns
Weight/Rep Logging
• Pre-fill with prescribed values
• Tap to edit (number pad)
• Or +/- buttons for quick adjust
• Save on tap-away or "Next"
Effort Rating
• 3 big emoji buttons: 😊 😐 😓
• One tap, no confirmation needed
• Appears after each exercise or every 2-3
Pain Flagging
• Body diagram or icon list
• One tap to select region
• One tap to select severity
• Auto-dismiss with confirmation
Navigation
• Bottom tab bar: Home, History, Settings (simple)
• Or swipe between workout screens (if linear flow)
Visual Design

Color Palette Suggestions
Primary
Warm, grounded earth tone (rust orange, deep teal, forest green). Conveys: stability, warmth, home, natural

Secondary
Complementary accent (lighter shade of primary). For highlights, progress indicators

Neutrals
Warm grays (not cold blue-grays). Backgrounds, text, borders

Alerts
• Success: soft green (workout complete)
• Pain/caution: amber/orange (not red - not scary)
• Hard effort: deep red/crimson
Avoid
• Neon colors (too aggressive)
• Pure black/white (too harsh)
• Baby pastels (too soft)
Typography
Hierarchy
• Large, bold for primary actions ("Start Workout")
• Medium for exercise names, instructions
• Small for secondary info (set count, tips)
Tone
• Friendly but not cutesy
• Clear but not clinical
• "Let's get started" not "Begin training protocol"
Iconography
Style
• Rounded, friendly (not sharp/angular)
• Consistent weight/stroke
• Functional over decorative
Icons Needed
• Dumbbell (equipment)
• Circular arrows (recurring/repeat)
• Body parts (for pain mapping)
• Calendar (history)
• Settings gear
• Heart/pulse (effort)
• Checkmark (completion)
